Support & FAQ

How do I post a truck or load?

How it works

LOAD MASR is one board for two sides — carriers post capacity, shippers post freight, and both sides search each other.

01

Create your account

Sign up as a carrier or a shipper, choose a plan, and you're verified in seconds.

02

Post what you've got

Carriers list a truck and its lane. Shippers list a load and where it needs to go.

03

Search the other board

Filter by lane, equipment, and pickup date to find your match, then contact them directly.
